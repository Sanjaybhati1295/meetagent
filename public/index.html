    Enterprise Security & Privacy

    Your Data Stays Yours. Period.

    We know meeting content is sensitive. MeetAgent is engineered with zero-compromise security principles:

    • 🔒
      Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS)

      Your meetings, transcripts, and account data are cryptographically bound to your user ID. No cross-tenant data leakage is physically possible.

    • 🛡️
      Zero Training On Your Conversations

      Audio is ingested in-memory and deleted immediately after processing. Your speech is never used to train public foundation models.

    • ⚡️
      Ephemeral Processing Pipeline

      Transcripts are processed via encrypted HTTPS endpoints with fast Groq and Gemini APIs designed for enterprise compliance.
